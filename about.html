About

Built by a chandler,
for chandlers.

WickWise started as a set of calculators I kept rebuilding for my own bench — fragrance ratios, burn time, wick sizes, pricing math. I put them on the web so other makers could use them too. Everything here is free, and everything you type into a calculator stays on your device.

What you'll find here

🕯️ Candle calculators

Batch math, fragrance loads by wax type, burn-time estimates, wick picker (cotton and wood), wax-melt ratios, dye amounts, and pricing that accounts for labor — not just "double the wax cost."

📖 Guides

Plain-language walkthroughs for beginners, choosing a wax, and working with fragrance oil — the stuff that usually takes months of trial and error.

🖼️ Maker gallery

What other makers are pouring. Made something you're proud of? Show it off.

Where this is going

WickWise is growing into a verified-seller marketplace — a place to buy candles from makers we'd vouch for personally, starting with our own pours. The calculators stay free either way. That's the deal.
