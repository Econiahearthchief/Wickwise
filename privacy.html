Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

WickWise (wickwise.shop) is a set of free candle-making calculators and guides. This policy explains what information we handle and how advertising works on the site.

Information we collect

  • Calculator inputs stay in your browser. Everything you type into our calculators is processed locally on your device. We don't receive it, store it, or transmit it anywhere.
  • Email addresses you give us directly. If you join a waitlist or email us, we keep your address only to reply or send what you asked for. We never sell it or hand it to third parties for marketing.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads that keep the calculators free. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

Our ads.txt file lists the advertising partners authorized to sell our ad inventory.

Analytics

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand which calculators people use, so we know what to improve. This data is aggregated and never tied to your identity.

Children's privacy

WickWise is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
