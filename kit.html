The other way in

Most beginners' eyes land on this first, and we get it — one box with a pouring pitcher, tins, wicks, stickers, and holders for about fifteen bucks. People start in different ways, and it's a legitimate way in.

Our honest take: we don't pour with this one. A pitcher setup means heating your wax in a water bath and watching the temperature like a hawk — look away at the wrong moment and you've scorched the batch. That's exactly why WickWise pours with the electric melter pot (Piece 1 above): set the temperature and it holds it. If the budget says start with the box, start with the box — just know the trade you're making.

See the all-in-one starter kit on Amazon →

Going this route? Add one cheap save most newbies never hear about: your pour pot sitting directly on the bottom of the water pot is metal on metal — and the wax right at the bottom of your pour pot scorches. Drop three 2-inch wooden blocks in the water pot first and rest the pour pot on them. The wood keeps the metal off the metal, and your wax thanks you.
