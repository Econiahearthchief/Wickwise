The WickWise Family

Never throw a candle away again.

Burn it down, ship the leftovers back, and we give that wax a second life as Family Blend — our recycled line at half the price of a new candle. Any brand. Any wax. If it burned, we'll take it.

1 · Burn it down

Enjoy your candle all the way to the bottom. Scrape out what's left — chunks, flakes, dusty bits, all of it counts.

2 · Ship it back

Box up your leftover wax and send it our way. Family members get 50% of the shipping covered once they've bought their first candle from us.

3 · It comes back as candles

Your old wax joins the Family Blend pool and comes back as recycled candles at half price. Nothing wasted.

Two ways to send wax

🕯️ Family members

Bought your first candle from us? You're family. We cover 50% of the shipping to send your used wax in.

Family membership ($4.99/mo) also covers outbound shipping on your recycled candles.

♻️ Just want it gone green

Not a member yet, but hate the thought of good wax in a trashcan? Ship it at your own cost and we'll keep it out of the landfill — it feeds the same Family Blend pool.
